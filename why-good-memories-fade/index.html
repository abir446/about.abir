Why good memories fade?

Abir Dutta November 5, 2026 3 min read

What can i call a good memory? The ones which we reminiscence and feel sad about? or the ones that we don't usually remember but we were happy in them?

I have mental yearly milestones, for example 2022 and 2024 hold a special place in my heart. In 2022, I just finished my school and got into college. I was always an introverted person and mixing into a group of strangers was quite challenging for me. It took me about 2 years just to know who I feel comfortable with. The start of something new felt like a spark for me. Also, Messi won the Fifa World Cup that year after one of the best matches in the football history. I was happy in 2022 but I don't usually remember the good moments from that year.

After spending two years to find my actual friend group in college, I felt lively and active. I started to become a little more talktative, friendly, and approachable towards strangers. Currently in November, 2026, I mostly interact with those friends of mine. However, I mostly spend my current days remembering and missing the feeling of being in 2024. I still try to hold on to those friends by repititive messaging and asking to meet them. However, I feel like I am the only one holding on. This month, I am travelling 413km just to meet a friend (how far are you willing to go just to remember your past self?).

My school friends are still in good touch with me. I don't miss them anytime. I think this is because, I stay myself when I am with them. The unfunny wannabe, the ragebaiter, the efootball strategist, and every other thing that I am, I am able to express myself with them. I play games, take part in sporting activites, and still hangout with them many times in a month. I don't have to hide half my personality with them.

This makes me feel like we only miss the memories, that were created by external people, where we were something else that we are every day. I don't miss my school friends because I feel like myself every and do not require my friends to feel what I feel with them. I hope it makes sense.

 we don’t miss places or events, we miss the specific versions of ourselves that only certain people could pull out of us.

However, I recommend having friend groups outside the common circle. It extracts the hidden personalities within us. To conclude, there is nothing called good memories, we just miss what we used to be.
